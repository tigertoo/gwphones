What it does

Desk phones that set themselves up

Yealink phones fetch their settings from the platform over HTTPS, each with its own credentials: line keys, busy lamps for colleagues, voicemail and firmware updates included.

Phone apps

Extensions on iPhone and iPad apps, set up by scanning a one-time QR code or tapping an emailed link, with push notifications for incoming calls.

Calls routed your way

Ring groups, voice menus with your own greetings, business hours and holidays, call park, pickup and forwarding, managed by each business from its own portal.

Voicemail and recording

Voicemail to email with the recording attached, and call recording with the notice callers hear.

Calls from the browser

Call links for websites and email signatures: visitors call the business from their browser, with no app or account, and with video as an option.

Secure by design

Encrypted calls and signalling (TLS and SRTP), each business walled off from the others, and a portal protected by two-factor codes or passkeys.
