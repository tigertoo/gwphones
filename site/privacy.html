Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

GW Phones is a hosted phone system for businesses in Ireland. This page explains what personal data the platform handles, why, who else sees it, how long it is kept, and your rights. Questions: info@amapforyourmoney.com.

Who is responsible

GW Phones runs the platform. For the businesses that use it, our customers, we are responsible for their account and billing details. For the calls those businesses make and receive, the business decides how its phone system is used (for example, whether calls are recorded), and we handle that data on its behalf and on its instructions. If you called a business that uses GW Phones, that business is your first point of contact; you can also write to us.

What we handle

  • Call records: the numbers calling and called, the extension, the time, how long the call lasted and how it was routed. These are needed to connect calls, to bill them and to look into faults.
  • Call recordings: only when the business has switched recording on for an extension; the business tells callers, and the platform can play them a notice first. Recordings are deleted automatically after the period the business sets (90 days unless it chooses otherwise).
  • Voicemail: messages left for an extension, and the caller's number. They can be emailed to the extension's user, as the business sets up.
  • WhatsApp calls: when you call a business on WhatsApp, Meta (WhatsApp's provider) passes the call to us with your WhatsApp phone number, or an identifier Meta gives in its place. We then handle it like any other call. Meta's own WhatsApp privacy policy covers what WhatsApp itself does.
  • Calls from a web page: when you call a business through one of its call links, the call's audio (and video, if you turn it on) passes through the platform. We don't ask for your name or account.
  • Accounts: for people who use the customer portal or a phone extension: name, email address, extension, a sign-in password (stored only as a hash), two-factor or passkey details (for a passkey, only its public key), and a record of sign-ins and changes made, for security.
  • Desk phones: a phone's hardware (MAC) address, model and firmware version, and the IP address it last fetched its settings from.
  • Billing: the business's name, email address and its statements.

We don't sell personal data, use it for advertising, or track visitors. This site uses no analytics. The customer portal uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and, if you choose, to remember a trusted device for 30 days.

Why, and on what basis

  • To provide the phone service a business has signed up for, and to bill it (performing our contract with that business).
  • To keep the service secure and working: spotting misuse, blocking repeated failed sign-ins, investigating faults (our legitimate interests).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping accounting records.

Who else sees it

Only service providers who help us run the platform, each for its own part:

  • Telecoms carriers, which connect calls to and from ordinary phone numbers.
  • Meta, for WhatsApp calls.
  • Microsoft, which delivers our emails (voicemail, setup links, statements).
  • Yealink, when a business sets up a Yealink desk phone to configure itself: the phone's hardware address and where it should fetch its settings.
  • Our server hosting provider.

Some of these providers may handle data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards that data protection law requires, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We also disclose data where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Recordings: for the period the business sets, 90 days unless it chooses otherwise.
  • Voicemail: until the extension's user or the business deletes it.
  • Call records and statements: for as long as we need them for billing and accounting, or as the law requires.
  • Accounts: while the business uses the service; removed after it leaves, apart from what we must keep by law.

Your rights

Under data protection law (the GDPR) you can ask to see the personal data held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive a copy to take elsewhere. Write to info@amapforyourmoney.com. Where we handle the data for a business (its calls and recordings), we'll pass your request on to that business and help it answer. You can also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Security

Calls and phone sign-ins are encrypted between the phones and the platform (TLS and SRTP), each business's data is kept apart from every other's, and the customer portal is protected by two-factor codes or passkeys.
